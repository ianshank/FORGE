## Technical Observations & Convergence Analysis

1. **Active Learning Signals**:
   - **Baseline Parity**: In the early episodes, the trained agent should perform on par with the random baseline (within the shaded 1-sigma region).
   - **Breakout Point**: Look for the episode number where the rolling reward consistently exits the random baseline's 1-sigma bounds. This indicates that the MCTS-guided policy improvement has updated the representation network with useful spatial features.
   - **Milestone Achievements**: Spikes in the reward distribution correlate to the agent securing high-value one-shot rewards (defined in `milestone_rewards.toml`) such as first wood harvesting or shelter validation.

2. **Action Distribution Shifts**:
   - As training progresses, the model shifts its prior probabilities from uniform random actions to highly targeted target actions (e.g., executing `break_block` or `craft` only when oriented towards appropriate resources).
